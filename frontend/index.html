How a request moves

  1. Request. Pick a resource and an hourly slot on a weekday, up to seven days ahead.
  2. Review. An administrator takes the request into review. You can cancel until a decision is made.
  3. Decision. It is approved, or rejected with a reason. One booking can be approved per slot, so competing requests are declined automatically.
  4. After approval. You can still cancel. If a room is needed for maintenance, the booking is cancelled and you are told why.
